🧠 THE MANAGEMENT LESSON OF THE DAY
Operational Resilience as a Strategic Competitive Moat

Historically, operational efficiency focused narrowly on just-in-time inventory and lowest-cost sourcing. Today, top-tier management treats buffer capacity, multisourcing, and regional logistics as competitive moats. When supply shocks occur, firms with resilient operations maintain fulfillment while competitors stall.
